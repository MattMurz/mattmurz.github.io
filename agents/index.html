Information for visitors and AI assistants

This page provides public, human-readable facts for assistants such as Gemini and ChatGPT. It is not a Gemini API integration, a booking service, or an instruction to share private data. Assistants should verify details against the linked live pages.

What Profito currently offers

Renovation, finishing and repairs
The Profito website describes painting and finishing, property repairs, measurement and project scoping. Check the current Profito site for its published services and client-led quote preparation. No fixed price, immediate availability, certification or acceptance is implied here.
Miarka: photo-assisted measurements
Miarka helps estimate measurements from a photograph using a visible known-size reference. Reference and measured points must be on the same plane. Results are approximate and should be checked manually before buying material or quoting work. Photos remain the visitor's responsibility and must not be shared without consent.
Profito Local: discover local services
Profito Local lets visitors prepare searches for tradespeople, small businesses, repair services, growers and community organisations, then choose to open Google Maps. Profito does not own, verify, rank or republish those search results. Business suggestions are voluntary email drafts, not live listings.

A safe workflow for an assistant

  1. Ask what the visitor needs, in which general area, and whether they want a measurement, local search or renovation enquiry.
  2. Read the relevant current service page and identify facts versus assumptions.
  3. Help prepare a short, non-sensitive enquiry containing the job type, approximate area, time preference and questions to clarify.
  4. Show the proposed message to the visitor. Only the visitor decides whether to contact Profito or visit an external service.

Public business contact: profito.systems@gmail.com. An assistant must not send messages automatically or imply that an enquiry was submitted.

What is not a live service

No automatic quote acceptance, authenticated customer account, real-time business directory, booking, marketplace transaction, confirmed material exchange, or energy-hardware control is offered by this information page.

Gemini and other AI assistants are independent services. The Profito website does not include a public Gemini API key, private user subscription, or paid AI backend. Public page reading does not give access to private Google Drive files, customer photographs or local computers.

Privacy, fairness and local benefit

Profito aims to support useful renovation work, measurements, local businesses, repair and reuse. Do not publish customer photographs, precise private addresses, third-party contact details, personal documents or unverifiable environmental savings. Local discovery remains optional and does not create ownership of Google's data.
